# What is OpenBioFigure?

OpenBioFigure is an open-source editor for scientific figures. It combines a vector canvas, editable scientific elements, a verified offline asset catalog, and provenance-aware exports in one local-first application.

## What you can create

Use it for experimental workflows, mechanism diagrams, cell interactions, microscopy panels, comparisons, timelines, simple charts, graphical abstracts, and multi-panel figures. Text, shapes, arrows, assets, and generated scientific elements remain individually editable.

## Who it is for

OpenBioFigure is designed for researchers, doctoral candidates, students, educators, biomedical teams, and authors who need clear figures without a mandatory account or proprietary project format.

## What makes it different

- **Vector-first:** SVG is the preferred editable export.
- **Attribution-aware:** placed catalog assets retain their source, creator, licence, and required credit.
- **Local-first:** editing, search, autosave, and export happen on your device.
- **No mandatory AI:** every core workflow is deterministic and works offline.

OpenBioFigure helps track licensing metadata; it does not provide legal advice.
