# Text, shapes, and connectors

Use text for short labels and explanatory annotations. Rectangle, ellipse, and line objects work well for simple regions, boundaries, and keys. Arrows communicate direction; connectors provide editable straight relationships between elements.

Selected objects expose applicable fill, stroke, stroke width, opacity, font size, font family, and text alignment controls. Group related parts before moving or scaling them together. Ungroup when you need to edit an individual component.

Connectors in the current release are straight editable lines. They do not yet anchor semantically to objects or route around obstacles.
