# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, safe, and harassment-free environment for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Focus criticism on ideas and work, not people.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Respect privacy, consent, attribution, and intellectual property.
- Correct mistakes openly and without retaliation.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualised attention, doxxing, deliberate intimidation, and sustained disruption are not acceptable. Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission is prohibited.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through an appropriate private GitHub channel. Do not disclose sensitive details in a public issue. The maintainer may remove content, close contributions, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the project.
